Todo: product overview

A personal to-do app for one person juggling work and home tasks across a phone and a laptop. Each task can be tagged, scanned at a glance, timed with a countdown and reminded by email, and it is the same list on every device.

Decision to approveA responsive web app with Google-only sign-in, colored free-form tags that make rows scannable, and per-task countdowns and email reminders that reach you even when the app is closed.

Already decided

DecidedTimer = a countdown that alerts
  • Set a duration, press start, get alerted at zero.
DecidedReminders = email
  • Sent at a chosen time, to your Google email.
DecidedSign-in = Google only
  • One button. No passwords, no profile screen.
DecidedOrganizing = free-form tags
  • Any number per task, each with a color.
DecidedCountdown end always emails
  • Banner and chime in every open tab, plus an email every time.
DecidedCountdowns: cancel only, no pause
  • Several tasks can run a countdown at once.
DecidedResponsive web only for v1
  • No native apps. A Google account is required.

Key choices

1. How does tag color make a row recognizable at a glance?

RecommendedRow stripe from the first tag, plus chips
  • One strong color per row, scannable from across the room
  • Reordering tags changes the stripe, so no extra setting
  • "First" is implicit; some users won't notice it can be changed
Colored chips only
  • No hidden rule, every tag treated equally
  • Small chips are hard to scan; a row with 3 colors reads as noise
User picks a "main" tag per task
  • Explicit and predictable
  • One more control on every task; empty for most tasks

The stripe is what makes rows "identifiable at a glance", and drag-to-reorder gives the control of a main tag without another field.

2. Does setting a due date add a reminder automatically?

No, reminders are always added by hand
  • No surprise emails
  • Users who set a due date often expect a nudge and won't get one
RecommendedYes, "1 hour before", easy to remove
  • The common case works with zero extra taps
  • Shown as a normal reminder you can delete
  • Some unwanted emails until people learn to remove it

The request says every task should have reminders, and a sensible default delivers that without extra setup.

PRD slices

Tasks at a glance

Add tasks, tag them, and scan the list: row layout, tag colors, due chips, views and filters.

Timers and reminders

Countdowns (start, cancel, alert) and email reminders, and what reaches you when the app is closed.

Accounts and sync

Google sign-in, staying signed in, sign-out, and the same list on every device.

Where slices meet: the row's countdown and reminder icons are placed by Tasks at a glance but defined by Timers and reminders. Live countdown sync across devices belongs to Timers and reminders. The email address reminders go to comes from Accounts and sync.

Out of scope for v1

  • Native phone apps and offline mode (responsive web only)
  • Pausing a countdown (cancel only)
  • Use without a Google account
  • Push, SMS or browser notifications; email and in-app only
  • Sharing tasks or lists with other people
  • Recurring tasks, subtasks and attachments
  • Any sign-in other than Google
