Accounts and sync

One person adds "Book dentist" on their phone at the bus stop and wants to see it on their laptop at work, with no password to remember and nothing to set up. This slice covers getting in, staying in, getting out, and the list being the same on every device.

Decision to approveOne Continue with Google button is the only way in; the first sign-in creates the account from the Google email, with no name or profile screen. A device stays signed in until 30 days without a visit. Every device shows the same tasks, tags, countdowns and reminders, and an open tab catches up within seconds of a change elsewhere. Responsive web only.

Rules and edge cases

  • One account per Google email. Signing in with a different Google account shows a different, separate list.
  • Reminder address is the Google email shown in the account menu. It can't be edited in v1; if the Google email changes, the next sign-in updates it.
  • Sign out signs out this device only, returns to step 1 and leaves nothing of the list visible in the browser. Other devices stay signed in.
  • Two devices edit the same task at once: the later save wins for that task, and the other device shows the winning version within seconds. No merge prompt.
  • Lost connection: a bar reads "You're offline. Changes can't be saved until you reconnect." Adding and editing are disabled until it clears; nothing is queued.
  • Session ended elsewhere (e.g. Google access revoked): the next action returns to step 1 with "Please sign in again." Any unsaved text in the add field is kept.
  • Phone and laptop get the same views; the layout reflows down to a 360px-wide screen.

Out of scope for this slice

  • Any sign-in besides Google, a profile or settings page, deleting the account (v1).
  • Offline mode and native apps.
  • Live countdown time-left across devices (Timers and reminders).
  • Row layout, views and tags themselves (Tasks at a glance).
Unsure about
  • "Sign out of all devices" is left out of v1. A lost phone stays signed in until 30 days without a visit, or until access is revoked from the Google account. Is that acceptable?
  • 30 days without a visit keeps a daily user signed in for good while ending forgotten sessions on shared machines. Shorter (7 days) is safer but means more sign-ins. Keep 30?
  • Later save wins can quietly drop an edit when two devices change the same task within seconds. Rare for one person, but tell us if you'd rather see a conflict prompt.
  • No offline editing means nothing can be added on a train with no signal. Fine for v1?
  • Platform "responsive web only" is still pending your answer on the overview; this page assumes yes.
