Timers and reminders

People forget tasks the moment they switch tabs or close the laptop. Any task can carry a countdown ("give this 25 minutes") and email reminders ("nudge me before it's due"), and the alert reaches the person even when the app is closed. For the same one person on phone and laptop as the overview.

Decision to approveOne countdown per task (several tasks can run at once) that can be cancelled but not paused; at zero a banner and chime on every open tab, and always an email. Up to 5 email reminders per task, with a removable "1 hour before" added when a due date is set.

Rules and edge cases

  • Several at once: yes. Each task has at most one countdown, and any number of tasks can run at the same time. Two ending together give one banner each, stacked.
  • No pause: a running countdown can only be cancelled. Cancelling sends nothing; to continue later, start a new one.
  • Marking a task done cancels its running countdown silently (no banner, no email) and stops its reminders. Reopening it restores reminders still in the future; the countdown does not come back.
  • Email at zero: always sent, once, at zero, whether or not the banner is seen or dismissed. "+5 min" starts a new 5-minute countdown, which sends its own email when it ends.
  • App closed: no banner or chime is possible, so the email is the alert.
  • Reminders: at most 5 per task, no two at the same minute, never in the past (the save button says "Pick a time after now"). Relative presets need a due date; without one only Custom is offered.
  • Auto reminder: added the first time a due date is set, unless that time is already past. Removing it is remembered; it is not re-added when the due date changes.
  • Due date moves: relative reminders move with it; any that would land in the past are dropped, with a note "1 reminder removed: time has passed". Removing the due date removes relative reminders and keeps custom ones.
  • Deleting a task cancels its countdown and all its reminders.
  • Chime only plays if the browser allows sound; the banner always shows.

Out of scope

  • Row layout and where the icons sit (Tasks at a glance); sign-in and the email address itself (Accounts and sync).
  • Push, SMS or browser notifications; recurring reminders; snoozing a reminder email.
  • Count-up timers, time tracking or reports of time spent.
Unsure about
  • Is 5 reminders per task enough? Picked to keep the editor short; easy to raise.
  • Should "+5 min" exist on the banner, or is Dismiss alone simpler? It is the most common reaction to a focus timer ending.
  • Is a 12-hour cap on a custom countdown right? Anything longer is better served by a reminder.
  • Should the "Mark done" link in the email work without a sign-in? Convenient, but anyone holding the email can complete the task.
