Tasks at a glance

For one person keeping work and home tasks in one list, checked many times a day, often on a phone. Today every task looks the same, so they open tasks just to learn what they are, and miss what is late. This slice makes adding a task one step and makes rows tell themselves apart.

Decision to approveAdd a task by typing a title and pressing Enter. Tasks carry free-form tags, each in one of 8 colors. A row shows a stripe in the first tag's color, the title, up to 3 tag chips then +N, a due chip (Overdue in red, Today in amber, otherwise the date), and fixed slots for the countdown and reminder icons. Four views, Today, Upcoming, All, Done, each filterable by one tag.

Rules and edge cases

  • Title: required, trimmed, 1 to 200 characters. Enter on an empty box does nothing. Long titles wrap to two lines then end in "…".
  • Due chip: date-only tasks are Today all day and Overdue from midnight. Timed tasks turn Overdue the minute the time passes ("Overdue · 09:00" today, "Overdue · Fri" for earlier days). Past dates may be set; the task is simply Overdue. Times use the device's time zone.
  • Colors: 8, shown with their names in the picker: blue green red amber purple teal pink grey. 8 is about the most a person can tell apart from a narrow stripe; more colors start to look alike, and the chips carry the name anyway. Colors may repeat across tags; new tags take the next color not yet used, then cycle.
  • Tags: names 1 to 24 characters, unique ignoring case ("Work" and "work" are one tag). Up to 10 per task. Renaming to an existing name is blocked: "You already have a tag called home."
  • Red and amber on due chips mean time, never a tag. A red tag stripe next to a red Overdue chip is allowed; the chip's word carries the meaning.
  • Filter: one tag at a time. It stays on when switching views and shows in the view bar until cleared. Deleting the filtered tag clears the filter.
  • Deleting a task: from the edit panel, with "Undo" for 5 seconds. No confirm dialog.

Out of scope for this slice

  • How countdowns and reminders behave, look inside their slots, and alert (Timers and reminders).
  • Sign-in, account menu and keeping devices in sync (Accounts and sync).
  • Search, sorting options, multi-tag filters, bulk edit, manual reordering of rows.
  • Recurring tasks, subtasks, notes and attachments (out of v1 per the overview).
Unsure about
  • Is a cap of 10 tags per task right? Rows show only 3, so 10 is generous; the cap mainly keeps the edit panel usable.
  • Should a task added while in the Today view get today's date? Without it, it vanishes from Today the moment you add it. Proposed: yes in Today, no date elsewhere, with a short "Added to All" note when the new task isn't in the current view.
  • The stripe rule (first tag wins, reorder to change) is still pending approval on the overview. If it changes, only the stripe and the drag row above change.
